Listed but Not Recognised: Diacritics and Label Provenance in African Language Identification In Preparation

Victor Miene

Target: ACL 2027

Tested 9 language identification tools on natural text in 20 African languages. A listed label can hold another language, and a correctly labelled class can fail when its training text is marked differently from how people write. Where a language is not recognised, tone marks push its text toward better-recognised neighbours like Yoruba, while placebo marks do not.
